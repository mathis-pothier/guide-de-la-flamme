Although the Misbegotten Catacombs isn't necessarily super hard to figure out on your own, it's not as obvious as some of the other puzzles I've stumbled upon so far. So, I figured I'd make a quick and easy guide in case anyone gets stuck. Enjoy. So, initially we're going to head straight and then take a turn to the left. And as you've probably noticed, I have cleared out most mobs in order to shorten the guide and save everyone some time. This door right here will be locked, so make sure to bring a lock pick. Then take another left and climb up this railing and you'll see the first button. Once we've pressed it, we're going to head back towards the entrance hallway to pick up the second button that we already passed. Stop right by these stairs and you'll see just enough of it in order to snipe it with your bow. Continue straight on and down the stairs and take a right by the door. Keep heading forward until you reach the corner of the room and the third button will be there waiting for you. With just one left to go, we backtrack a couple of steps and take a right turn, use our grappler to end up on top of this platform that has a hidden pathway behind this breakable wall. Shoot an arrow at the explosive and then proceed inside and up the stairs where you will have to deal with some enemies. Now, go ahead and press the last button and then make your way towards the unlock chamber. So, what do you end up getting for doing all of this? Pipe meets bullwark.
