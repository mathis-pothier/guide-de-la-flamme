[music] Here's what they have to say about it. &gt;&gt; So, we absolutely want to keep high view distance, but we were not happy with the detail loss in the distance. Converting all this voxel data into triangles is a big bottleneck, especially when we want to increase the detail seen at a distance. Instead of converting voxel data into triangles, we now create little bricks filled with a compact surface representation called signed distance fields. We can stream them in or create them on demand based on GPU feedback. The process of creating the bricks is much faster than creating triangle meshes because we use the signed distance field as an intermediary step to mesh creation. The small bricks are very nicely suited to calling mechanisms, so we submit less work to the GPU than before. Additionally, it lends itself to using a visibility buffer internally, which reduces the memory bandwidth because we don't write out so much data all the time when multiple bricks overlap. The brick render is quite a bit faster, depending, of course, on GPU, while allowing much higher detail at a greater distance. &gt;&gt; They have also been optimizing shadows, and here's what they have to say about that. &gt;&gt; Our new system, called World SDF, combines all objects into a central data structure, leading to just a single intersection lookup at any world position. It also optimizes data bandwidth by a factor of 10, while reducing the number of nodes we need to traverse considerably. Performance-wise, we get a two times ray tracing speedup in simple scenes such as the top of a mountain with no trees and models around, up to three to four times when trees and forests are nearby, [music] and even reaching eight times speedup in thick bumble fields. &gt;&gt; Point light shadows are being reworked as it is a very expensive process, and they are looking to reduce the shader process time, which means less time idling in the main menu waiting for them to compile.
