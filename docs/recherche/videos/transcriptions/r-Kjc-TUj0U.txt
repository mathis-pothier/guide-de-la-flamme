# Enshrouded Quest Guide - The Ancient Spires
# Viktor Vox | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r-Kjc-TUj0U | sous-titres: en

Greetings friend board. I am Victor Vance. Welcome back to the channel. Today we will complete the quest the ancient spires. To start this quest speak to Oswald the blacksmith and select the scale and ancient spire dialogue option. You must have completed the quest clear the elixir well to access this quest. For a guide on completing that quest click the box in the upper right hand corner now and return to this video once you've completed it. In order to get to the quest area you must have completed the quest grappling hook. From our starter base we will go north towards the large tower in the distance until we arrive at a bridge. Instead of going under the bridge we will use our newly acquired grappling hook to go over it. When you approach this gap in the bridge you'll have to do a jump glide to get across. Ascend the iron gate and once at the top avoid three bear traps. A scavenger ranger will be ahead to defeat. Follow the dirt road to the north at the crossroad continue following the path north. The dirt road ends at a garrison blockade. But we can use our grappling hook to climb over it. On the other side of the blockade we must defeat two scavengers one ranger and one warrior. A silver chest and a lore book are near the gate. We are going to follow the dirt road that leads north. We'll take the left at the fork in the road to follow the path along the cliff wall. This pathway will lead you to a large tower that you saw at the beginning of the quest. Ascend the tower stairs and use the teleporter pad in the middle of the tower to begin your ascent of the tower. When you arrive at the first floor of the tower head southwest to push a glowing button. This unlocks the door to your left where you can loot a silver chest. Return to the teleporter pad and ascend the stairs. Use your grappling hook to swing across, open the double doors. Use the grappling hook to jump across the ledge at the end of the hallway. Use the teleporter pad to ascend to the second floor. Before using the teleporter to the next floor, ensure that you've broken every pot in the tower and they contain valuable loot. When you arrive on the second floor, jump towards the iron gate and a climb up it. Ascend to the ledge on your left side. Use your bow and strike the button. This opens the door to your right where you can loot a silver chest. Jump to the ledge to the west twice. Take the stairs down and use the teleporter to take yourself to the third floor. Once on the third floor, open the double doors before you. Carefully time the spikes and proceed down the hallway. The end of the hallway, open the double doors. Inside, open the double doors to your right. You'll see a double locked door. Head right and use the iron gate to climb up the wall. Carefully move over the spike floor and push the button to your right. Do a jump glide across the hallway to loot the chest on the other side. Jump down and return to the hallway. This time open the other double door. Use the platform to jump over the lava pit and loot the chest across the room. Do a sprint jump to the other platform and use the iron gate to ascend the ledge to the top. Push the button at the top and glide your way back down. Return to the double locked door and loot the golden chest. Jump back across the lava pit and use the teleporter pad to ascend to the top of the tower. Speak to the flame in the tower center to complete the quest. If you enjoyed this guide, please press the like button and leave a comment let me know how I can improve these videos.
