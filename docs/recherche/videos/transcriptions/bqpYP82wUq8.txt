# Enshrouded 1.0 Update Changes EVERYTHING!
# GP | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bqpYP82wUq8 | sous-titres: en-orig

Enshrouded is officially leaving early access on October 15th, and Keen Games recently revealed some major combat overhaul changes for 1.0 release. We are getting a massive rework for mage classes, brand new necromancy summons, upgraded wand combos, archer survival buffs, and another raised level cap and new biome. This is such an exciting time for Enshrouded, and even better is it's releasing on PlayStation 5 on October 15th as well. Currently, Xbox release is scheduled for early 2027 to ensure the game's complex terraforming and building mechanics run smoothly across both Xbox Series S and X. Here is everything you need to know about the new combat system before launch. Starting with mages, magic is getting a huge expansion with nine brand new spells. For crowd control and utility, you have entangle to pin down elite mobs, mana drain to constantly leech energy back into your pool, and two massive area of effect nukes, blizzard and ball lightning. Now, if you love summoner builds like using the skull summoning vessels, we finally have dedicated necromancy and support summons, fire, ice, and shock totems alongside summonable hollow skeletons. Necromancy is something that players have been asking for for a long time now. Ever since we had the skull summoning vessels introduced, I've wanted full on skeletons and enemies. But even better, the entire spell progression has been retuned. There are now 22 tier seven eternal spells that don't consume spell ammo. You can now upgrade eternal spells just like weapons and armor to unlock custom perks, including reduced mana costs and faster casting spells. We are definitely going to be checking all of this out at the October 15th release, so make sure to hit subscribe so you don't miss out. Now, wands have also been reworked with a built-in heavy attack that pierces through enemy lines and interrupts attacks on lighter targets. You can now unlock aerial wand attacks for midair area damage, wand evasion attacks, and improved soft and hard lock targeting that lets you target explosive barrels directly. Now, for those of you that are more akin to hybrid magic, the new blast and cast skill gives your staff attacks a 50% base damage boost for 10 seconds following a wand critical strike. Now, this is a lot of changes and buffs for the mage, but for those of you that aren't quite into mage gameplay, but prefer more the archer style of play, you are also getting some solid buffs. You now have puncture for bonus pierce damage, barrage to restore bow durability on hit, survival instinct to restore shroud time whenever you kill infested enemies, and three new upgradeable eternal arrows: fire, shock, and bone. These are some big combat changes coming in 1.0 after we got some big combat changes for the melee characters in previous updates. Now, for some other gameplay changes coming, King Games have introduced new variants of some enemies to help match players stronger loadouts. This includes enemy factions like the fell and scavengers that will be getting heavy and elite variants with updated aggression patterns and movement animations. But don't worry, you'll be able to get even stronger in the 1.0 release because Keen Games are once again updating the max player level cap, which will be going from 45 to 50 along with equipment scaling up to level 55. Now, if you played this game right from the very start in early access, you'll realize how much of a huge increase player levels up to 50 and weapons up to 55 is so different from the very early days. So, if you're the kind to try and farm those high levels of your favorite weapons like me, get prepared to do it all over again. But an important note here, because of all the new skills and changes, the skill tree will be completely reset once again. I think this happened with the previous update a while back. So, if you want to remember what you currently have to compare so you can either get those back or check what's different, make sure to take some screenshots. So, with the level cap jumping straight to 50, you are going to need a lot of XP to unlock these high-tier skills and equipment level 55 gear on day one.
