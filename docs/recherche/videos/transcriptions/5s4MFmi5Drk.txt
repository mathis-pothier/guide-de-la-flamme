# Enshrouded: Everything Major Added Since Launch
# IGN | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5s4MFmi5Drk | sous-titres: en

When Enshrouded entered early access back in January 2024, it was already an ambitious game. There was survival, building, gliding, terraforming, action RPG combat, and a giant fantasy world cloaked in a deadly fog called The Shroud. And of course, like most early access games, there was the promise that this was just the beginning. King Games has been dedicated to improving Enshrouded over the past 2 and 1/2 years, and they haven't just added a few weapons and called it a day. Whether you're keen to dive back into Unshrouded's 1.0 release in October or you're starting fresh, here's a look at everything new that's been going on in EmberVale since launch. Enshrouded launched into early access with its core action survival RPG loop firmly established. You play as a flame in the cursed realm of Emberale that's been overtaken by a corrupting mist called the Shroud. It's your job to clear the shroud and restore Emberale to its former glory. This involved traveling to each region and awakening NPCs, defeating bosses, and cleansing the land of the Shroud. One of the most notable features that Enshrouded launched with was an incredibly in-depth building system. Instead of building your base out of pre-made structures, you construct it yourself using Enshrouded's incredible Voxil engine. You can do this alone or with up to 16 friends. And Shrouded's first major update was the Hollow Halls, which introduced several massive dungeons designed for multiplayer. Each region had its own hollow hall, giving you new places to explore, find new furniture items and building blocks, and test out your fighting skills with your friends. The hollow halls also introduced a brand new NPC to invite back to your base. And when you got home after a long day of slaying skeletons, you could actually sit in your furniture, which was a small but appreciated addition. The next update introduced the Blackm, a brand new swamp-like region filled with new enemies, materials, another new survivor, and the introduction of music. The update included a handful of instruments that you could find and play by yourself or with friends. Playing music in Enshrouded is fun, of course, but it's also useful and boosts your rested buff, so you stay rested for longer. [music] The Melodies of the Maya update also introduced a new weapon type, dual wielded daggers, which was a lifesaver for dexterity based builds. The Souls of the Frozen Frontier was one of Enshrouded's biggest updates, introducing yet another region and new mechanics that drastically changed the game. The snowy peaks of the Albene summits brought with them a higher level cap, dynamic weather, and the ability to both farm animals and keep them as pets. Non-hostile animals can be found and brought back to your base. When cared for correctly, these animals provide useful resources that can be used for crafting or cooking. Farming and cold weather management are two updates that drastically increased the depth and complexity of Embervale, but the updates didn't stop there. Other notable additions in the Souls of the Frozen Frontier update were improved gliding mechanics and the ability to use your grapple hook in combat. The Pact of the Flame update really honed in on the multiplayer aspect of Enshrouded, introducing in-game voice and text chat. Building mechanics were also enhanced and players were able to adjust things like how Warner structure looks, allowing for the creation of things like ruins and abandoned buildings. Thraws of Twilight introduced updates to the Shroud and areas of interest within it along with a weapon gem system. Weapon gems can be found from powerful enemies and installed at special gem forges that are hidden within the shroud itself. These gems provide additional bonuses and perks to your weapons and can be upgraded, making combat preparation far more interesting. There was also a new NPC introduced here. The barber specializes in tweaking a character's appearance, giving players more flexibility around the way they look. If you'd been exploring Ember Veil since Enshrouded's release, you'd probably seen everything there was to see on land. Wake of the Water solved that problem by introducing water simulation. Players can swim, dive, and build underwater, providing a whole new region to explore called the Veil Water Basin. This is the point where fishing was introduced as well, giving players even more ways to interact with water from land. In April 2026, King Games released what they called the final major early access update before 1.0. This one didn't introduce a new biome or any major new mechanics, but instead was focused on enhancing Enshrouded's building blocks. This involved overhauling combat mechanics, introducing heavy attacks, and a new focus resource that can be built up and used to unleash powerful weapon specific abilities. Enemy behavior was made more sophisticated, making your foes much smarter and suspicious of any sounds you make. If an enemy hears you, it can approach to investigate the sound, making stealth and tactical positioning much more important. The skill tree was also rebuilt. gear finally became upgradable and adventure sharing was introduced, making Unshrouded a more fleshed out multiplayer experience. Enshrouded has been in early access for over 2 and 1/2 years and has evolved to become a far more polished and immersive experience since then, but of course, there is still more to come. Enshrouded will finally be leaving early access on October 15th, 2026. And in classic King Games fashion, there are plenty of new updates to look forward to. The 1.0 0 release will finally finish the main story line. New traversal options have been teased. The game will get a massive quality of life pass. And best of all, Enshrouded will finally get a console release. You can experience these updates and more on October 15th on PC and PS5 with an Xbox Series X/S release slated for spring 2027. And that's every major thing added to Enshrouded for now at least. King Games is currently hard at work gearing up for the full release, but until then, there is plenty to keep you busy and enshrouded.
