# Enshrouded Quest Guide - A Story of Fire
# Viktor Vox | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8I3DCRH0FY | sous-titres: en-orig

Greetings, Flameborn. I am Victor Vox. Welcome back to the channel. Today, we will complete the quest A Story of Fire from beginning to end. To start this quest, speak with the Flame. You must have completed the First Flame Altar Base Improvements quest to access this quest. For a guide on completing that quest, click the box in the upper right-hand corner now and return to this video once you've completed it. To begin this quest, fast travel to Spring Lands Ancient Spire. We'll jump off the west side of the tower and land at the farmstead below. When you land at the farmstead, you must defeat three wolves. This location is called Harvest Homestead. It is a great spot to gather red mushrooms, corn, and tomatoes. After gathering the food supplies and defeating the wolves, head west to Woodgard. Head up the stairs to this building, but turn right instead of entering. Start taking the cobblestone road. This will aggro a swarm of bees. After defeating the swarm, continue traveling on the cobblestone road until you reach this crossroad with a well in the center. Take a left on the dirt road moving towards the south. Continue on the dirt road until you reach a lone building. The dirt road ends at a T intersection, and we will take a right. After you pass the revive point, you'll encounter five wolves. Defeat them to continue. Follow the dirt road until you see a column of orange light. Head to this column of light. Press E on the glowing obelisk to complete this quest. If you enjoyed this guide, please press the like button and leave a comment letting me know how to improve these videos.
